How the check works

Audio fingerprint. Most AI music generators build the final audio with learned upsampling layers. Those layers leave very faint lines spaced evenly across the spectrum, a regular comb that recorded and mixed music doesn't have. CNVS averages the spectrum of the 30-second preview, removes the music's own shape and measures how strong and how regular any such comb is. This runs on your device.

Release pattern. Mass-produced AI catalogues tend to release far more often than people do, often weekly, across many genres. CNVS reads the artist's release history from Apple Music and flags unusual volume. A busy release schedule on its own doesn't mean a song is AI-made.

Limits. A preview is a short, compressed clip. Heavy processing, re-recording or new generators can hide the fingerprint, and songs that only use AI for part of the work (vocals, mastering, lyrics) may not show it at all. Treat the result as one piece of evidence alongside what you know about the song.
